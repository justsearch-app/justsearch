Round 3 baseline: `a74847e434` (product change `183f2a3d9e`). Candidate: **the repair commit containing this map**. Round 3 changes the empty proof and adds final-pointer checks; its new execution results are identified separately below.

Round 2 historical evidence: baseline `7a6ab81ea11279008a853fa5574b11da4b051c02`, candidate `a74847e434`, whose production code is identical to `f00f25ff30`. Round 2 adds the four required source labels and repairs the help fixture, not a product or saved-value change. Test source digests and individual baseline/candidate cases are in [round-2 results](installed-model-activation-round2-results.json). [Repaired evidence](installed-model-activation-repaired.json) records 12 executed candidate cases and 12 baseline assertion failures. [Selected-suite evidence](installed-model-activation-preservation.json) records 176 executed candidate cases. An additional 45 existing recovery cases passed on both builds, bringing unique candidate cases to 229 (234 on the baseline, including five help-enumeration cases only selected there). None was skipped. Some selected classes contain both repaired and preservation methods; those are distinguished below. **Classification correction:** five encoder negatives refused on the baseline too; only their diagnosis assertions differed. They are P, not R. Within Encoder, only `generationCapChangesPublishThroughBWithoutCallingAsQueryOnlyOwner` is R. The round-2 producer’s 12 before-failures include these five diagnosis-only failures; they do not establish 12 behavioral repairs.

The help fixture now calls `bindBootRootBindings(List.of())` before startup, exactly as `EngineRoot` does even for an empty registry. Without this binding, configured-root volume seeding cannot begin. The private evidence profile had put temporary files on a filesystem without stable volume identity and concealed this missing setup. Normal `/home` test resources expose it. The fixture uses the current `v4` help marker. The standard `test` invocation and the CI `ciUnitTest2` invocation pass on the candidate and fail on the baseline at the missing `justsearch-help` field assertion, not a timeout. All assertions and volume protections remain.

`R` means new/repaired behavior: fails before for the assertion under test, passes after. `P` means behavioral preservation; `PASS → PASS` requires both executed checks to pass. Diagnosis-only baseline assertion failures are explicitly named below and are not baseline passes. Rows labelled intended preservation but unverified have no paired live result. **Partial** identifies a passing narrower check; it does not certify the full scenario. **Unverified** means the required real-system check has no result on one or both revisions. No configured, queued, cached, or unexecuted case counts as a pass. An independent verifier still owns acceptance.
