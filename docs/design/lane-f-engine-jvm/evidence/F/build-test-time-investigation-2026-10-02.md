### 1 — Capture the native post-pointer witness on the migration thread

**Evidence and cause.** The case takes 199.580s (`modules/app-engine/build/test-results/test/TEST-io.justsearch.app.engine.EngineNativePointerBootMutationTest.xml:5`).
